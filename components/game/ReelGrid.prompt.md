The 3×3 reel window: gold frame, nine `ReelCell`s, animated payline sweeps on top.

```jsx
<ReelGrid
  cells={[{symbol:"tent"},{symbol:"gem",state:"win"},{symbol:"crown"}, …]}
  paylines={[1]}
  assetBase="../../"
/>
```

Only three columns and three rows — the game is deliberately small and readable on a phone.
