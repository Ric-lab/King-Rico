The chunky gold-rimmed pill button used for every text action in Fortune Circus; `cta` is the single green primary on a screen, `gold` is secondary, `ghost` is tertiary on purple.

```jsx
<Button variant="cta" size="lg" full onClick={spin}>King Rico</Button>
<Button variant="gold">Comprar moedas</Button>
<Button variant="ghost" disabled>Sair</Button>
```

Labels are uppercase and short (1–3 words). Never place two `cta` buttons in one view — the green belongs to spinning and winning.
